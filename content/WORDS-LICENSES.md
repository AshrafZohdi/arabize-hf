# Word lists for the daily code

`words-ar.json` and `words-en.json` are the real words the daily code accepts as guesses. They are generated by `scripts/build-game-words.mjs`, which keeps words of 3 to 9 letters and normalises their spelling. They are loaded into the database and do not ship in a release build of the app.
